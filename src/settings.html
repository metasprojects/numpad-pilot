Numpad Pilot

Press numpad decimal to move the cropped Reel without leaving the game. While moving, use numpad 8 / 2 / 4 / 6, then press decimal again to save its position. Outside move mode, 4 / 6 changes Reel volume by 2%.

Numpad shortcuts

Use Electron accelerator names, such as num2 or Control+Alt+R. Each shortcut must be unique.
